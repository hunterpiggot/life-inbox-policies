Life Inbox — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Program name and description

Life Inbox is a private personal-assistant text messaging program operated by Hunter Piggot (sole proprietor) for two members of one household. Members text the Life Inbox number with reminders, calendar items, grocery and shopping notes. The program replies with confirmations, short clarifying questions, grocery and price summaries, and price-drop alerts for items the member saved. It does not send marketing messages.

Opt-in

Only the two pre-registered household members can use the program. A member opts in by texting the Life Inbox number (for example, texting START). Messages from any other number are ignored.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with use, typically a few messages per day.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Text STOP to stop receiving messages at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Text HELP for help, or email hunterpiggot@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
