Life Inbox — How to Opt In (SMS)

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Life Inbox is a private personal-assistant text messaging program operated by Hunter Piggot (sole proprietor) for the two members of his household. This page is the program's public call to action and describes the only way to opt in.

Step 1: Text the keyword

From your mobile phone, text the keyword START to (719) 249-5404.

By texting START to (719) 249-5404 you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Life Inbox: confirmations of reminders and calendar items you send, short clarifying questions, grocery list and price summaries, and price-drop alerts for items you saved. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per day). Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time. See the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Step 2: Confirmation

You receive this confirmation message:

Life Inbox: You are opted in to household assistant texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Who can join

The program is limited to the two members of the operator's household. Messages from any other phone number are not processed and receive no reply.

Opt out and help

  • Text STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE) to opt out. You will get one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Text HELP for help, or email hunterpiggot@gmail.com.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and opt-in data are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See the Privacy Policy.
