Life Inbox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Life Inbox is a private SMS assistant operated by Hunter Piggot (sole proprietor) for the members of a single household. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • The mobile phone numbers of the two household members who have opted in.
  • The content of text messages and photos those members send to the Life Inbox number, and the replies sent back.

How information is used

Messages are used only to perform the request the sender made: for example adding an event to a household calendar, keeping a grocery or pantry list, or tracking prices of saved items, and to send the related confirmations, questions and alerts.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Message content is processed only by the service providers needed to run the assistant (the SMS carrier/provider and the calendar provider) and is never sold.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on privately owned equipment. Photos are deleted after 180 days and message text after one year. Messages are deleted from the SMS provider after they are received.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. To have your data deleted, email hunterpiggot@gmail.com.
